EXAM BLUEPRINT

Understand KLEE Before You Prepare

KLEE is a Computer-Based Test (CBT) evaluating speed, accuracy, and legal reasoning. Master the distribution of 120 questions & 360 marks.

Aptitude for Legal Studies

126 Marks

42 Questions • Highest Weightage (Legal principles, reasoning & maxims)

General English

108 Marks

36 Questions • Reading comprehension, grammar & idioms

GK & Current Affairs

81 Marks

27 Questions • Polity, international affairs, science & history

Arithmetic & Logic

45 Marks

15 Questions • Number system, series, blood relations & logic

The KLEE paper tests something very specific—not how much law you know, but how precisely you think under pressure. Our programme builds that precision through structure, repetition, disciplined practice, and continuous improvement—not by overwhelming students with endless content. We don't just prepare students for an entrance examination; we prepare future advocates, legal professionals, entrepreneurs, and leaders.
